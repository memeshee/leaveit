Leave It

A morning filter for a backlog you have decided not to read.

Every item below is a real issue from a real repository. For each one the model answers a single question: does this need a maintainer today? Items it defers are held by a durable Temporal fuse, so a decision to ignore something cannot be lost by a crash, a reboot or a closed laptop lid.

What the filter buys you

11%

of the held-out stream never reached the human, while wrongly deferring 11 of the 235 items that genuinely needed someone within 24 hours. A perfect ranker could defer 68%; deferring everything would defer 100% and miss everything.
